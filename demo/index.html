Results

On 392 real pop recordings (MulTTiPop test set), AMTFlow beats the model it drives, MuScriptor without instrument tags: +3.1 family F1, +1.1 exact-instrument F1 and +1.0 instrument-agnostic F1. It assigns notes to the right instruments and finds more of them. Each module can be swapped, so AMTFlow's accuracy rises as instrument identification and AMT backbones improve.

Listen: no tags, all tags, AMTFlow

MuScriptor writes only the instruments it is told about. Without instrument tags, and with all of them, it often picks the wrong ones. These instrumental Jamendo tracks are examples. Switch between the three transcriptions while the recording plays.

Each example plays a 30 s excerpt of the recording above the MIDI tracks. Press S on a track to hear it alone, or M to mute it. Starting one player pauses the others.

The recordings are vocal-removed Jamendo tracks from the ATTM challenge test set, shared under the artists' Creative Commons licences. AMTFlow ran the MIDI-REPA pipeline (--config paper), which was built for instrumental music. All three versions use MuScriptor large. "All tags" gives MuScriptor every one of the seven families. Instruments listed under "Jamendo tags" were tagged by the uploader on Jamendo, not by AMTFlow. All transcriptions come from models and contain errors.
